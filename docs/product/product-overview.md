## 1. Executive Summary
RIGHT FIT is an AI-powered Career & Opportunity Intelligence Platform that bridges the gap between individuals seeking professional opportunities (job seekers, students, career switchers, freelancers) and organizations in need of talent or solutions.

Unlike traditional job boards that flood users with thousands of unfiltered, often phantom listings, RIGHT FIT applies deterministic skill matching, multi-source intelligence, broken-website auditing, and community pain-point detection to present actionable, verified opportunities.

## 2. The Problem
1. **Opaque & Inefficient Job Search:** Job seekers spend 20+ hours a week tailoring resumes for black-box ATS algorithms without knowing why they were rejected.
2. **Ghost Postings & Misleading Salary Data:** A significant fraction of posted jobs are inactive, stale, or offer compensation far below market expectations.
3. **Manual Freelance Prospecting:** Freelancers and agencies manually crawl Google Maps and directories to find businesses with broken or non-existent websites.
4. **Scattered Tech Opportunities:** Startups post technical bottlenecks and "help wanted" requests across Reddit, GitHub, and niche communities without a central discovery engine.

## 3. Core Value Propositions
* **Explainable Matching:** Users receive a fit score (0–100%) paired with explicit rationale traces showing matched and missing skills.
* **Unified Opportunity Stream:** Access full-time employment roles, local business freelance pitches, and startup technical opportunities from a single dashboard.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Safety:** Applications and pitches are drafted with AI assistance but require human approval before dispatch.
* **Holistic Career Intelligence:** Offers compensation calculators, equity cliff analysis, STAR interview prep briefs, and offer negotiation strategy.

## 4. Target User Personas
1. **Students & Freshers:** Looking for first roles, internship matches, and automated resume gap audits.
2. **Experienced Professionals:** Targeting mid-to-senior engineering/product roles, compensation breakdown, and negotiation points.
3. **Freelancers & Agencies:** Seeking local business clients needing web design, SEO, and technical modernization.
4. **Indie Hackers & Open-Source Contributors:** Seeking startup pain points and GitHub issues needing paid developer intervention.

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
* Match Quality Ratio: Percentage of recommended roles marked "relevant" by users (> 80%).
* Time-to-Application: Reduction in hours spent customizing resumes and cover notes (-60%).
* Client Conversion: Success rate of freelance pitches generated through LeadHunter (> 15% reply rate).
